# Chatbots in the Counseling Room, Seamspace in the Classroom — Where Do You Meet a Child's Heart?

![A 1:1 chatbot room and a whole-class classroom — two seats inside one school](/assets/images/chatbot-vs-seamspace-children-mental-health/01.jpeg)

Children's mental chatbots are a fast-growing category. 24/7 anonymous access, clinician-designed conversations, crisis monitoring — increasingly capable solutions are emerging. This post looks at one role in particular — **the role of mental chatbots** — alongside the role Seamspace plays: **a classroom-activity tool**.

## 1. Where mental chatbots shine

When a student feels in distress and reaches out on their own, a chatbot is there 24/7, anonymously. A widely known example is **Alongside** ([alongside.care](https://www.alongside.care/)), an AI mental wellness coaching chatbot for K-12 schools. Their AI assistant "Kiwi" uses clinician-designed dialogue techniques, monitors every interaction for suicidal ideation and abuse signals, escalates crises to a clinical review team, and alerts school personnel — all backed by ESSA Level 3 efficacy certification.

Mental chatbots shine when a school needs to provide *"a 24/7 anonymous companion for students who recognize they need help."*

## 2. The bigger everyday — the students who never reach out

The catch is that only students who *recognize* their distress and *reach out* will open the chatbot. Students who don't realize they're struggling, those uncomfortable asking for help, the quiet ones, the "I'm fine" responders — their hearts rarely show up in chatbot data. Out of a class of 30, perhaps one or two are frequent chatbot users.

The bigger question schools face every day is this — *"How can we meet the hearts of all 30 students gathered in one place during class? How can we naturally meet the majority who don't reach out?"*

This is not the territory of 1:1 counseling. It belongs to **the classroom-activity space**.

## 3. Where Seamspace sits — themed writing in the classroom

Seamspace is not a chatbot. It is a **classroom-activity tool**.

- **Themed writing activities**. Teachers introduce a topic during class (e.g., "The moment that stayed with you most today," "What you couldn't say to a friend"), and the entire class responds in their diaries. It integrates naturally into Korean language, ethics, creative-experience, or morning-circle classes.
- **Every student joins**. Not just those who asked for help — the whole class participates simultaneously. Even the quiet students, even those who would have answered "I'm fine," show up in the data naturally.
- **AI auto-analysis + reply**. Student writing is classified into 46 emotions, and four AI counselors reply privately to the student. The personal space stays protected.
- **Teacher dashboard**. Class emotional flow becomes class-management data. It even guides the choice of the next writing topic.
- **Online-offline integration**. Diary + Mind Journey board game + Emotion Friends keychains.

In this space, Seamspace meets **the entire class** naturally during class time — before any student needs to reach out for help.

## 4. Two roles, two problems — and the time of school

Mental chatbots shine in 24/7 anonymous care for students who recognize their distress. Seamspace shines in meeting the whole class together during class time. Schools face both kinds of work, and the two complement each other.

But most of a school's eight-hour weekday is spent in **class**. When the hearts of all 30 students can be met during that class time, the foundation of the school's emotional landscape is built. When meeting students *before they reach out* becomes routine, the crisis situations chatbots address become fewer.
