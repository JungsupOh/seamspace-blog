## Understanding Your Emotions is the First Step to Managing Them

In our busy daily lives, truly recognizing and expressing our emotions can be surprisingly difficult. For young students especially, the question "How am I feeling right now?" can feel awkward or confusing. seamspace supports social-emotional learning (SEL) starting from this simple, meaningful question.

![alt](/assets/images/daily-emotion-checkin-one-minute/01.png)

## Five Emotional Categories, Made Simple and Clear

seamspace's **AI 마음일기** (AI Mood Diary) classifies complex emotions into five distinct stages. From happiness and sadness to surprise and pride, and further to solitude, sympathy, jealousy, shame, and timidity—each emotion is brought to life with adorable characters.

Even younger students can intuitively identify their feelings, and the descriptions for each emotion naturally expand their emotional vocabulary. That vague sense of discomfort that couldn't be named suddenly finds expression as 'solitude,' 'sympathy,' or 'jealousy.' This clarity is where emotional awareness truly begins.

![alt](/assets/images/daily-emotion-checkin-one-minute/02.png)

## Just One Minute a Day Fits Any Schedule

We often hear: "My child refuses to write in a journal," or "It's too much to remember every day." We understand these real challenges. That's why we created an emotion check-in that takes just one minute.

Select a date, choose an emotion, and optionally add a single sentence of reflection. That's it. Your emotions appear visually on the calendar, and over time, you can spot patterns in how you feel. No pressure, no overwhelm—just meaningful daily practice.

## See Your Emotional Journey at a Glance

![alt](/assets/images/daily-emotion-checkin-one-minute/03.png)

Every check-in you record is organized and analyzed through different views: your journal, group insights, and mind stories. AI 마음일기 goes beyond simple note-taking to help you *understand your emotional patterns* and *build better emotional awareness*.

Whether you tend toward procrastination or find yourself constantly busy, a one-minute check-in shouldn't feel like another chore. With seamspace, recording today's feelings becomes a simple, powerful habit. Those small daily moments of reflection add up to something profound—truly knowing yourself.
