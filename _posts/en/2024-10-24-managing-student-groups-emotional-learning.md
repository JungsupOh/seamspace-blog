## Real Case Study: Analyzing Emotions by Math Achievement Level

![Emotional data comparison by math learning level](/assets/images/managing-student-groups-emotional-learning/02.jpg)

When one class divided students into high-performing and slower-paced math learners and analyzed their emotional data, a striking discovery emerged: **many high-performing students in math were experiencing negative emotions.**

This reveals a critical insight: academic achievement alone doesn't guarantee psychological well-being. Students who appear outwardly successful may still be struggling internally with stress, anxiety, or other emotional challenges.
