## Do We Really Only Feel a Few Emotions?

In the movie 'Inside Out,' children experience 5 core emotions (joy, sadness, anger, disgust, and fear), expanding to 9 emotions in adolescence. But what about reality? Our actual emotional lives are far more nuanced and complex.

Every day, we experience emotions in shades and degrees. The warmth of happiness differs from the lightness of hope. The sting of loneliness feels different from the weight of despair. To capture this rich emotional landscape, **seamspace has created an emotion dictionary with 46 distinct emotions**—one that truly reflects the full spectrum of human feelings.

![alt](/assets/images/46-emotions-dictionary-seamspace/01.png)

## How Are Emotions Classified?

seamspace organizes emotions across three meaningful dimensions:

**1. Emotional Valence: Positive, Neutral, and Negative**
- Positive emotions: happiness, gratitude, hope
- Neutral emotions: calmness, solitude
- Negative emotions: anger, loneliness

**2. Emotional Direction: Inward and Outward**
- Inward-focused: emotions centered on the self
- Outward-focused: emotions arising from relationships and connections

**3. Emotional Energy: Measured by Temperature**

![alt](/assets/images/46-emotions-dictionary-seamspace/02.png)

## Understanding Emotions Through Temperature

What makes seamspace unique is expressing each emotion as a **temperature**. Inspired by the Mood Meter framework in psychology, we quantify emotions based on Pleasure and Energy levels—translating them into intuitive temperature values.

For instance:
- **Happiness**: 9°—high pleasure, high energy, warmest joy
- **Gratitude**: 7°—steady, genuine warmth
- **Sympathy**: 3°—cool yet meaningful
- **Anger**: -7°—intense, burning frustration

![alt](/assets/images/46-emotions-dictionary-seamspace/03.png)

When you journal in AI 마음일기, each emotion's temperature combines to create **your daily emotional temperature**. This goes beyond simple mood tracking—it provides a multidimensional understanding of your emotional state.
