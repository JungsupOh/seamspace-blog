# Discover Your Heart's Rhythm With Monthly Emotion Reports

Our emotions flow like the tide—sometimes calm, sometimes turbulent. We know that feeling only good or only bad isn't healthy. Just as weather changes day to day, so does our heart. **seamspace's AI Emotion Diary** makes it easy to see these emotional patterns at a glance.

![Emotion Trend Graph](/assets/images/monthly-emotion-report-discover-your-heart/01.jpg)

## Your Heart's Story Over One Month

Every emotion you record isn't just a number—it's a trace of your life, a wave of your mind. The monthly report provides two key insights into your emotional landscape.

Through **Emotional Temperature and Mindfulness Index**, you can see how your feelings shifted over the past 30 days and identify patterns. Rather than reading raw numbers, visual graphs let you reflect: *"Ah, that's when I was struggling,"* or *"I was really happy then."* You experience your month's emotional journey as a continuous narrative.

## Discovering Your True Self Through Data

![This Month's LBTI Personality Analysis](/assets/images/monthly-emotion-report-discover-your-heart/02.jpg)

One of the most compelling features of AI Emotion Diary is **LBTI (Life Balance Type Indicator) Analysis**. It's fundamentally different from typical personality tests.

Traditional personality assessments rely on subjective answers: *"I think I'm an extrovert."* But seamspace takes a different approach. We analyze your personality **based on actual behavioral data**. The emotions you've recorded over the past month combine to show your genuine personality type more objectively.

The personality discovered this way might differ from what you thought. And that difference becomes your gateway to deeper self-understanding.

## Emotional Health Check: The Foundation of Wellness

![Emotional Status and Frequency Analysis](/assets/images/monthly-emotion-report-discover-your-heart/03.jpg)

Your monthly report transcends simple statistics. Through **This Month's Top Emotions** and **Heart Health Score**, you discover: *"Which emotions did I feel most this month?"*

Whether it's anger, joy, sadness, or anxiety, identifying which feelings dominated your month illuminates your current psychological state. This goes beyond self-reflection—it becomes the first step in **practical emotional management and mental wellness**.

## Begin Your Emotional Journey With seamspace

The AI Emotion Diary's monthly report doesn't end at *"I recorded my emotions."* It's a process of answering deeper questions: *"Who am I really?"* and *"Is my heart healthy right now?"*

Once a month, read your heart's patterns and discover your authentic self. seamspace walks this journey with you.
