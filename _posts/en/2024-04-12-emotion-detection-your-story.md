# Understanding the Emotions Hidden in Your Story

Small moments happen every day. Sometimes they carry complex, mixed emotions. Relationships with friends, unexpected situations, and the various feelings that come with them.

**seamspace's AI mind journal** doesn't just search for specific words in your story. It understands the full context and discovers the genuine emotions hidden within your narrative.

![Mind journal emotion tags](/assets/images/emotion-detection-your-story/01.png)

## Finding up to 3 emotions from 46 possibilities

Our emotions are complex. You might feel not just "worry," but also "loneliness" and "regret" all at once. seamspace identifies up to three emotions in your story, reflecting your feelings more accurately.

## Both positive and negative emotions can coexist

Just like wanting to spend time with a friend but feeling disappointed they're busy, our emotions are rarely just one thing. seamspace recognizes these complex feelings and shows you the true, mixed nature of your heart—where positive and negative emotions live together.

![Emotion analysis results](/assets/images/emotion-detection-your-story/02.png)

## Understanding yourself better

What if the emotions seamspace identifies differ from what you thought? That's a wonderful opportunity for growth. Could your expression be clearer? Or might there be deeper emotions you haven't yet recognized in yourself?

## Mental health indicators to reflect on yourself

Based on your story, seamspace provides four mental health indicators: **stress, anxiety, depression, and feelings of inferiority**.

![Mental health indicators](/assets/images/emotion-detection-your-story/03.png)

⚠️ **Important**: These indicators are not medical diagnoses. Use them only as a reference. If you need professional support, please consult with a qualified professional.

## Record your daily emotions and grow

With seamspace's AI mind journal, you move beyond simply writing—you experience **understanding your emotions and growing as a person**. How was your day today? Share your story with seamspace and discover yourself anew.
